## Flow

1. An administrator adds a team member with the **interviewer** role (Settings → Team).
2. A programmer assigns the interviewer to a published survey (survey → Interviewers), optionally
   with a target.
3. The interviewer opens the app (address shown in the Interviewers tab, `CAPI_APP_URL`), signs in
   once online, and the app downloads the live version of every assigned survey.
4. Interviews run on the device. Answers are saved to IndexedDB after every page; an interview can be
   continued after the app is closed, or ended early (synced as `partial`).
5. Finished interviews sync when the app opens online, when the connection returns, or with
   **Sync now**. The server decides the final outcome (see ADR 0008) and the app shows it.

## Sync contract

`POST /api/capi/sync` (interviewer session, CSRF header for cookie sessions):

```json
{
  "deviceId": "uuid generated by the app",
  "interviews": [
    {
      "clientId": "uuid",
      "surveyId": "...",
      "versionId": "...",
      "answers": { "AGE": 40, "GENDER": 2 },
      "startedAt": "ISO 8601",
      "finishedAt": "ISO 8601",
      "durationMs": 240000,
      "language": "en",
      "endedEarly": false
    }
  ]
}
```

Response: `{ "results": [{ "clientId", "result": "stored" | "already_synced" | "rejected", "status"?, "reason"? }] }`.

Milestone 12 adds, when the survey records them:

```json
{
  "location": {
    "start": { "lat": 51.5007, "lon": -0.1246, "accuracy": 9, "at": "ISO 8601" },
    "end": { "lat": 51.5009, "lon": -0.1247, "accuracy": 12, "at": "ISO 8601" }
  },
  "audio": "recorded"
}
```

`audio` is `recorded`, `declined` or `unavailable` (no microphone or permission). Photos and the
recording are uploaded first, one file per call:

`POST /api/capi/captures` (multipart: `clientId`, `surveyId`, `ref`, `kind` = `photo` | `audio`,
`file`). `ref` is the photo answer's value (a UUID generated on the device) or `audio`. Repeating an
upload returns `{ "stored": false }`. The server links the files to the interview when it syncs.

Reasons you may see: `T_<termination id>`, `capi_ended_early`, `capi_incomplete`,
`capi_invalid:<page>` (stored as technical error with the raw answers), `quota_full_at_sync:<quota>`,
`quality:<flags>`.

## Location, recording and photos (Milestone 12)

Set under Quality & fieldwork → Face-to-face capture (part of the draft, published with it):

- **Location**: off, when the device allows it, or required. The app takes the position when the
  interview starts and when it ends; it is rounded to about a metre on the server. Required: the
  interviewer cannot start without a location (the app explains how to allow it).
- **Recording**: off, or after the respondent agrees. Before the first question the app shows the
  consent text to read out and two buttons: _Respondent agrees: start recording_ and _Respondent
  declines_. While recording, the top bar shows **● Recording**. Audio is saved on the device
  every 10 seconds; if the app is closed, what was recorded so far is kept and the interview
  continues without recording.
- **Photo questions** work offline: the photo is shrunk, its metadata removed, and kept on the
  device until sync.

At sync the app uploads files first, then the interview; an interview whose files could not be
uploaded yet waits for the next sync (the app says so). Once the server has the interview, its files
are deleted from the device. Reviewers see the location (as coordinates, no map service), the
distance between start and end, the recording and the photos in the review queue.

Checks: `gps_missing`, `gps_moved` (over 1 km) and `interviewer_overlap` (two interviews by the same
interviewer at once). See [data quality](data-quality.md).

## Transcripts of recordings (Milestone 17)

With `TRANSCRIPTION=deepgram`, a `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` and a `DEEPGRAM_MODEL` (the Deepgram model to use;
there is no default), admins and programmers see **Transcribe N** under Quality & fieldwork →
Photos and recordings. It asks first, because the recordings leave SampCode: each recording without a
transcript is sent to Deepgram in a background job (up to 200 per job), in the interview's language
(the primary subtag, `fr` of `fr-CA`). The text appears under the recording in the review queue,
marked as machine transcription.

- Same access as the recording: only the team, via the review queue. Erasing a recording, or the
  retention period deleting it, deletes its transcript too.
- A recording Deepgram refuses is listed in the job result with Deepgram's reason; the others are
  kept. If none could be transcribed, the job fails with the reason.
- A worker without transcription configured leaves these jobs for one that has it.
- **Status: tested against a local stand-in of Deepgram's API (in the API and browser tests), not
  against Deepgram itself.** Try a short recording on staging first.

## Blueprint phase 3: evidence, supervision and appeals

- **Interview areas** (`settings.capi.geofences`): circles where interviews may take place. The app
  warns an interviewer who starts outside; the server re-checks the start location and flags
  `outside_geofence`. GPS accuracy counts in the interviewer's favour, up to 1 km.
- **Page timestamps and interruptions**: the app records when each page was shown and for how long,
  and each time the interviewer left the app (2 s or more) or pressed Pause. Pauses do not count toward
  the interview length. More than 5 interruptions or over 15 minutes in total flags `interrupted`.
  Reviewers see both under the interview's evidence.
- **Signature and barcode/QR questions**: see `docs/adr/0031-blueprint-fieldwork-and-security.md`.
- **Supervisor dashboard** (Interviewers tab): productivity and quality per interviewer, exceptions,
  a map of start points (no map tiles), back-checks and appeals.
- **Back-checks**: draw a share of each interviewer's completes; record verified, unreachable or
  failed. Failed rejects the interview through the quality review.
- **Appeals**: interviewers appeal a rejected interview once from "Results and appeals" in the app;
  a supervisor keeps the rejection or reinstates the interview (if its quotas have room).
